% This deliverable has presented a structured catalogue of fourteen use cases for the TenSyGrid Toolbox, organised into two groups. Use cases UC-01 to UC-06 are supported by an existing web-based Stability Monitor prototype and cover the essential stability-analysis workflow: from loading a grid model and running a small-signal eigenvalue computation, through interpreting the results via a dashboard, eigenvalue plots, critical mode tables, and bus voltage checks, to assessing the impact of N-1 contingencies with actionable recommendations.

Use cases UC-07 to UC-14 extend this foundation towards more advanced operator needs: scenario comparison, trend tracking, critical element ranking, damping controller placement guidance, renewable penetration assessment, automated report generation, centre-manifold analysis of zero-eigenvalue modes, and basin-of-attraction estimation for large-disturbance security margins. These proposals will be presented, discussed, and prioritised during the forthcoming workshop with network operators.

% The discarded or deferred use cases --- real-time monitoring, transient stability, voltage collapse, optimal power flow with stability constraints, and automated control tuning --- represent important but currently out-of-scope functionalities. They are documented here to provide a complete record of the design-space exploration and to inform potential future extensions of the toolbox.

The immediate next step is the operator workshop, where the use case catalogue will be validated and enriched with real-world operational experience. The outcome of the workshop will define the development priorities for the next iteration of the TenSyGrid Stability Monitor, ensuring that the toolbox addresses the most pressing stability-assessment needs of European transmission system operators in an increasingly converter-dominated grid.
